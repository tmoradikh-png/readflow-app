ReadFlow Support

We're here to help.

ReadFlow is a calm PDF reader that reflows documents to fit your screen and can read them aloud. It is made by Urmia Works, operated by Urmia Holding AS.

Contact support

Common questions

  • How do I open a document? Tap the ＋ button on your shelf and pick a PDF or Word file. ReadFlow copies it into the app so you can reopen it anytime from your library.
  • How do I remove a document? Long-press a document on your shelf and confirm. This deletes the copy stored on your device.
  • Does it remember where I stopped? Yes. ReadFlow saves your reading position and continues from there next time.
  • Can it read scanned PDFs? ReadFlow can run OCR on scanned pages so they can be read and spoken aloud.
