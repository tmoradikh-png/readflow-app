ReadFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

ReadFlow ("the app") is made by Urmia Works ("we", "us"), an independent software studio operated by Urmia Holding AS. ReadFlow turns your PDF and Word documents into a clean, reflowed reading view, reads them aloud, and can summarize or explain them with AI. We are committed to collecting as little information as possible. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Information we process

  • Documents you open. When you add a PDF or Word file, the file is sent to our servers to extract its text (and, on paid plans, to run OCR on scanned pages). A copy of the file you open is also stored privately on your own device so you can reopen it from your library. We do not browse, share, or sell your documents.
  • Text for AI features. When you use an AI action (summarize, explain, simplify, key points, or ask a question), the relevant portion of the document text is sent to our servers and to our AI processing provider (OpenAI) to generate a response.
  • An anonymous app identifier. ReadFlow does not require an account or login. To manage subscriptions and fair-use limits, the app uses a randomly generated, anonymous identifier that is not tied to your name, email, or real-world identity.
  • Basic technical data. Standard server logs and anonymous, aggregate usage counts (for example, how many AI actions were used in a month) that keep the service running, secure, and within plan limits.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email address, contacts, or social accounts.
  • No precise location data.
  • No selling or renting of your data to anyone.
  • No cross-app advertising trackers.
  • Text-to-speech playback is generated on your device; your reading audio is not recorded or uploaded.

How long we keep it

Documents are processed to extract their text and are not retained as browsable files on our servers. To keep the app fast and reduce cost, we may temporarily cache extracted text and AI results, keyed to the content of the document, for a limited time. The copy stored on your device stays until you remove that document from your library, which deletes the stored file.

Payments and subscriptions

Optional subscriptions are sold and processed by the Google Play Store (and, in the future, the Apple App Store). We receive confirmation of your subscription status linked to the anonymous identifier above. We never see or store your card details.

Third parties

We use a small number of service providers strictly to operate the app: cloud hosting for our servers, and OpenAI to process text for AI features. These providers handle data only on our instructions. App store providers handle purchases.

Children

ReadFlow is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS. We restrict access to our systems and keep only what is needed to run the service.

Your rights

You can clear your on-device library at any time from within the app. To ask about the data associated with your anonymous identifier, or to request its deletion, contact us at support@urmiaworks.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the app? Write to support@urmiaworks.com.
