You pinch, you zoom, you lose your place. Reading long documents on a phone shouldn't be a fight.

👆

PDF pages are fixed-layout. On a phone, that means constant pinching and scrolling to read a single line.

📍

Where was I?

No way to listen

Features

Read and listen, your way.

Everything you need to make long documents actually enjoyable on your phone.

Clean, reflowed text

readFlow extracts text from PDFs and Word docs and reflows it into beautiful, phone-friendly typography. No more pinch-zooming. Common page numbers, headers, and watermarks are filtered out when possible.

Listen your way

Three voice options to suit your needs: your phone's built-in voice, rF AI voice downloaded to your device, or premium Cloud AI voice on paid plans. Text highlights follow along as you listen.

AI reading help

Stuck on a dense paragraph? Ask for summaries, explanations, simplified text, key points, or ask a specific question about what you're reading. Available on AI plans.

OCR for scanned pages

Scanned PDFs don't have to be off-limits. readFlow can extract text with OCR on AI plans. Progress saves automatically, so large scanned books can continue later.

Multilingual support

Read documents in many languages. OCR supports English, European languages, Arabic, Persian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and more. Quality varies by language and source file.

Voice

Three ways to listen.

Choose the voice that fits your reading moment.

Built-in

Phone voice

Uses your device's installed voices. No cloud cost, no download needed. Great for long listening sessions without draining your data.

On-device AI

rF AI voice

Premium

Cloud AI voice

Higher-quality cloud voice for approved languages on paid plans. Capped by monthly allowance and quality-tested language by language.

A few things to know

readFlow works best with clean, text-based PDFs and Word documents. Scanned, image-heavy, or damaged files may require OCR and may not always convert perfectly.

AI answers are generated from your document text and may miss context or make mistakes. They're designed for reading assistance — always verify important information from the original.

Voice quality and availability vary by language, device, installed voices, and plan. Cloud AI voice is capped by monthly allowance and enabled only for quality-tested languages.
